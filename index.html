SkyStrike Arena — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 (replaces the version of 24 October 2026)

SkyStrike Arena ("the game") is a game published by Lunaro ("we"). It plays offline; it also has optional multiplayer: on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot (since 1.1.0) and online over the internet with a room code (since 1.1.4).

What the game collects

We collect nothing. The game does not send any personal information to us, and does not sell or share it. Only when you choose ONLINE multiplayer does it use the internet, as described below.

  • There are no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The only outside service is Cloudflare, which connects the phones in ONLINE rooms (below).
  • There are no accounts, no logins and no in-app purchases.

Local multiplayer (optional)

When you choose MULTIPLAYER and host or join a room, your phone connects directly to the other phones in that room, over your own Wi-Fi network or a phone's hotspot. Nothing goes through the internet or to us.

  • What the other phones in the room receive: the player name you type (up to 14 characters; please don't use your real full name), your game version, and the live state of the match (your soldier's position, aim, shots, health and score).
  • Finding rooms: a phone that hosts a room announces it on the local network about once a second, so nearby phones can list it. The announcement contains the room code, the host's player name, the number of players, the mode, the map and the game version.
  • Nothing is stored: this data is only used while the match runs and is not saved by the game on any phone (the name you type is saved only on your own phone, for next time).
  • Permissions: the game asks Android for INTERNET (Android requires it for any network connection, even a local one) and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to find your phone's Wi-Fi or hotspot address). Local multiplayer does not use the internet; online multiplayer does (above).

Online multiplayer (optional)

When you choose HOST ONLINE, or join with a 6-letter online room code, the game uses the internet:

  • Our room service (run for us by Cloudflare, Inc., as a service provider) hands out the room code and introduces the phones in a room to each other. To do that it receives each phone's IP address and the technical details needed to connect (WebRTC connection offers), but no name, no game data and no account. It keeps nothing after the room closes; Cloudflare may keep short-lived technical logs under its own privacy policy (https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
  • The other phones in your room connect directly to the host's phone, so they learn its IP address (and the host learns theirs), and receive the same things as in local multiplayer: your player name, your game version and the live state of the match.
  • Relays: when two phones cannot connect directly (some mobile networks block it), the match passes through a Cloudflare relay. It is encrypted (WebRTC) and not stored.
  • Online play is encrypted in transit (WebRTC). Nothing is stored after the match.

Updates

When the game starts on a phone, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available and, if so, shows Google Play's own update screen. This check is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; the game sends nothing to us.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (missions completed, stars, medals, difficulty, controls, sound, the blood & gore setting, your multiplayer name) in a file on your device. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes
