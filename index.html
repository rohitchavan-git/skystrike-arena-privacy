SkyStrike Arena — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

SkyStrike Arena ("the game") is an offline, single-player game published by Lunaro ("we").

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device information.

  • The game has no internet access: it requests no Android permissions at all.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party data services.
  • There are no accounts, no logins and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (missions completed, stars, difficulty, controls, the blood & gore setting) in a file on your device. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: open an issue at https://github.com/rohitchavan-git/skystrike-arena-privacy/issues (a free GitHub account is needed).
